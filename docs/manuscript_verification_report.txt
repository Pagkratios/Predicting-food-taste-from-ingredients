ON THE 29.8 SUSPICION
---------------------
The corpus mean salt across all 70 recipes is 29.8143 -- it rounds to 29.8
exactly. RP68's own hybrid prediction is 29.882, which rounds to 29.9, not 29.8.
At one decimal place the manuscript's figure matches the dataset-level
aggregate and NOT the per-recipe prediction.

This is not fully conclusive, and the reason matters: the two candidates are
only 0.07 apart, because the hybrid model regresses toward the mean and RP68's
actual salt (29.0) sits essentially at the corpus mean. The hybrid number also
comes from LassoCV under sklearn 1.9.0 and could shift by a few tenths under the
version originally run -- the same drift explains 20.867 vs 20.6 and 14.548 vs
14.4.

CONCLUSION: the evidence FAVORS a dataset-level leak but is not proof. What IS
certain is that 29.8 is a pre-optimization quantity under either reading, and
that no post-optimization salt prediction exists in the repo.
